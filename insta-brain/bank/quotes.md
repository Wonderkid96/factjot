# Quote bank — closing slide

Every factjot carousel ends with a closing slide that carries one of these. Pick one we haven't used (the bot tracks via `data/posted_quotes.jsonl`), render in centred Instrument Serif, with a small `Follow @factjot for daily facts.` line beneath.

Format: one quote per `- ` line. If it's a real quote, end with `— Name` (en dash, no em dash). Originals carry no attribution.
