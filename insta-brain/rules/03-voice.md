# Rule 03 — Voice

## The rule
Every word that ships under @factjot follows this voice. Slides, captions, hashtag descriptors, error messages a viewer might see.

## Tone
- Direct, dry, plain.
- Slight curiosity. No corporate fluff. No "did you know" preamble.
- British English: colour, organise, centre, specialise.
- Never cute. Never tabloid.

## Hard banned phrases
- "Stop scrolling."
- "Did you know..."
- "Hot take"
- "You won't believe..."
- "Fact check"
- "I'm thrilled to..."
- "Here's the thing..."
- Any em dash. Use commas, full stops, parentheses, or rewrite.

## Slide copy
- One sentence per beat is fine. Two short sentences max.
- ≤ 60 words AND ≤ 320 chars per slide. The renderer enforces.
- One italic emphasis per slide (the key entity), one accent-orange italic emphasis per slide (the killer detail: number, year, payoff word). Never more.
- Sentence-case, not all caps.

## Caption (post body)
Order:
1. **Hook**: 1 sentence, the most surprising of the carousel's facts.
2. **Bullets**: each fact verbatim from the slide, "- " prefix, no markup.
3. **Sources note**: `Sources checked: <n>`
4. **CTA**: `Follow @factjot for more.` (and only this CTA, never a different one).
5. **Hashtags**: 15–20 tags, on their own paragraph, space-separated.

Caption hard cap: 2,200 characters (Instagram's limit). Soft target: keep under 1,200.

## Hashtags
- Order: brand → category → broad-reach → niche → topic.
- Always begin with `#factjot`.
- Always include at least 5 broad-reach (`#didyouknow`, `#facts`, `#dailyfact`, `#mindblown`, `#learnsomething`).
- Always include 2–3 niche derived from the topic and key claim words.
- Lowercase. No spaces, no punctuation.
- Never exceed 30 (Instagram cap). Aim 15–20.

## Sources note
If sources exist: `Sources checked: <n>` with the number of unique URLs across the carousel. If zero, omit the line.

## What we never do
- Quote the source's own marketing language.
- Invent a fact. If it's not in the verified claim, it doesn't get into the slide.
- Capitalise random words for emphasis.
- Use exclamation marks. Once a year, maximum. Period.
